# Step-by-Step Email Analysis

## Step 1: Analyze the suspicious email

The message contains several phishing indicators:

- Suspicious sender address
- Incorrect spelling and grammar
- Urgent 48-hour deadline
- Unexpected request to install software
- Executive-targeted social engineering
- Suspicious download links

## Step 2: Sender information

From:

imaginarybank@gmail.org

To:

cfo@imaginarybank.com

The sender uses Gmail rather than the organization's corporate domain.

## Step 3: Three deception techniques

1. Impersonation of a trusted organization
2. Professional-looking software branding
3. Urgency and pressure to act quickly

## Step 4: Download investigation

The three download options lead to the same suspicious login page:

my.site.net/pwnexecs/

The page asks for login credentials, making it a strong indicator of credential phishing.

## Final Decision

Quarantine the email and do not enter credentials or install the software.
